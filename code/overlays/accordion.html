How do I get started?
Pick an idea, open a blank canvas and press play. The first step is always the smallest one.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Everything runs on your device, so it keeps going on planes, trains and very slow Wi-Fi.
What happens to my old drafts?
Nothing gets lost. Old versions stay in your history, so you can always travel back and borrow a good idea.
Can I start with just one project?
Of course. Most great things started as a single small one.
